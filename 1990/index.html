1990 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1990 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

1990 Ken Griffey Jr. card prices range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $1,450 for the 1990 Topps Base #336 in PSA 10.

Has any 1990 Ken Griffey Jr. card price moved recently?

Yes — the 1990 Donruss Learning Series #8 in PSA 8 most recently sold for $75 on August 27, 2026, Buy It Now, up 103% from $37 before that.
