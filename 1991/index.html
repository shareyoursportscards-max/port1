1991 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1991 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

1991 Ken Griffey Jr. card prices range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $4,500 for the 1991 Topps Desert Shield #790 in PSA 10.

Has any 1991 Ken Griffey Jr. card price moved recently?

Yes — the 1991 Score The Franchise #858 in PSA 10 most recently sold for $279 on August 22, 2026, Buy It Now, up 80% from $155 before that.
