1992 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1992 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

Based on eBay sales tracked here, 1992 Ken Griffey Jr. cards range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $5,226 for the Donruss Elite Series #13 (/10,000) in PSA 10. Exact value depends on the specific card, insert, and grade.

What is the most valuable 1992 Ken Griffey Jr. card?

Among tracked sales, the highest price for a 1992 Griffey card is the Donruss Elite Series #13 (/10,000) in PSA 10 at $5,226.

What is a raw (ungraded) 1992 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

A raw copy of the Donruss Elite Series #13 (/10,000), the year's top card, has sold for $412 — raw prices vary a lot by card and condition.
