1994 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

Based on eBay sales tracked here, 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. cards range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $3,040 for the Finest Refractor #232 in PSA 9. Exact value depends on the specific card, insert, and grade.

What is the most valuable 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card?

Among tracked sales, the highest price for a 1994 Griffey card is the Finest Refractor #232 in PSA 9 at $3,040.

What is a raw (ungraded) 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

A raw copy of the Finest Refractor #232, the year's top card, has sold for $866 — raw prices vary a lot by card and condition.
