1995 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1995 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

Based on eBay sales tracked here, 1995 Ken Griffey Jr. cards range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $3,000 for the Pinnacle Base #128 (Bubble Gum) in PSA 10. Exact value depends on the specific card, insert, and grade.

What is the most valuable 1995 Ken Griffey Jr. card?

Among tracked sales, the highest price for a 1995 Griffey card is the Pinnacle Base #128 (Bubble Gum) in PSA 10 at $3,000.

What is a raw (ungraded) 1995 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

Raw 1995 Griffey cards tracked here have sold anywhere from $2 to $700, depending on condition and how scarce the insert is.
