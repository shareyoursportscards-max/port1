1996 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1996 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

Prices for 1996 Ken Griffey Jr. cards range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $8,000 for the Select Certified Mirror Blue (/45) in PSA 9. Exact value depends on the specific card, insert, and grade.

What is the most valuable 1996 Ken Griffey Jr. card?

The most valuable tracked 1996 Griffey card is the Select Certified Mirror Blue (/45) in PSA 9, which has sold for $8,000.

What is a raw (ungraded) 1996 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

Raw 1996 Griffey cards tracked here have sold anywhere from $2 to $4,496, depending on the card and how scarce the insert is.
