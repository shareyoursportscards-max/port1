1998 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1998 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

1998 Ken Griffey Jr. card prices range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $32,433 for the Donruss Crusade Red (/25) in PSA 8.

Has any 1998 Ken Griffey Jr. card price moved recently?

Yes — the Bowman Chrome International Refractor #33 in PSA 10 most recently sold for $4,000 on August 4, 2026, up 150% from $1,600 before that.
