I put together the 50 biggest Ken Griffey Jr. card sales from the 1990s this year. Number 50 sold for $3,875. Number 1 sold for $240,000. I've been tracking every Griffey sale since the summer, and a few of the biggest ones come from earlier in the year. The really big cards just don't sell that often.

I'm not going to go through all 50 here. The full countdown is in the video. These are the ones that stood out to me.

## No. 1: 1998 E-X2001 Essential Credentials Now, PSA 8

!card 1998|1998 E-X2001|Essential Credentials Now (/10)

Sold for **$240,000** on July 24, 2026. The Essential Credentials Now cards were numbered to just 10. Nothing else on the list comes close.

→ [1998 E-X2001 prices](/1998/e-x2001/)

## No. 2: 1997 E-X2000 Essential Credentials #40, PSA 10

!card 1997|1997 E-X 2000|Essential Credentials #40 (/99)

Sold for **$132,000** on August 28, 2026. Numbered to 99. The regular Credentials version, numbered to 299, is also on the list at No. 8 for $39,600 in a PSA 10.

## No. 3: 1998 Metal Universe Precious Metal Gems, PSA 8

!card 1998|1998 Metal Universe|Precious Metal Gems (/50)

## What the list shows

- The 50 sales run from **$3,875** to **$240,000**. 18 of them are $10,000 or more, and 13 are $20,000 or more.
- **35 of the 50** are from 1997, 1998 and 1999. 1999 has the most with 15, then 1997 with 12. There's nothing from 1990 or 1994 on the list.
- **31 of the 50** are PSA 10s, 14 are PSA 9s, 4 are PSA 8s, and one is a raw card: a 1996 Ultra Thunderclap Gold Medallion, inserted 1 in every 720 packs.
- 21 of the 50 cards are serial numbered.
