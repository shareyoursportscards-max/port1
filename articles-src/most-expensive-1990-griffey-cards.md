1990 is Griffey's second year, and for most collectors it's the year they actually own: Topps, Donruss, Fleer, Score, Upper Deck, Leaf — all of it was everywhere. Which makes the top of this list more interesting than you'd expect. These are the ten most expensive 1990 Griffey sales I track, ranked by price. Every one is a real sale, nine on eBay and one on Fanatics Collect.

## 10. 1990 Donruss #365 — PSA 10

**$295** best offer (Sep 14)

The second-year Donruss base card. PSA 10 population is 547 out of 5,896 graded — about one in eleven. The red borders chip and the centering is all over the place, which is why a card that was in every shoebox in America still costs $300 in a 10. (Sold again at $300 two weeks later.)

→ [1990 Donruss prices](/1990/donruss/)

## 9. 1990 Topps Major League Debut #46 — PSA 10

**$305** on eBay auction, 22 bids (Aug 11)

From the Major League Debut boxed set. PSA 10 population is 1,405 — about three times the Donruss card — so it's an easier 10 to find, but it still cracks the top ten. Since the video it has traded between $329 and $430, so it's probably underpriced here.

→ [1990 Topps prices](/1990/topps/)

## 8. 1990 Leaf #245 — PSA 10

**$380** on eBay auction, 30 bids (Oct 4)

The iconic 1990 Leaf base card. Leaf was the premium set of 1990 — the one people actually tried to get. Thirty bids says there's no shortage of buyers; the $380 was a slight dip from the week before, and it's back at $385 as of November.

→ [1990 Leaf prices](/1990/leaf/)

## 7. 1990 Bowman Tiffany #481 — PSA 10

**$525** on eBay (Jul 23)

Tiffany means the glossy factory-set version, far scarcer than the regular Bowman. The regular 1990 Bowman #481 PSA 10 sells for about $160; the Tiffany is three to four times that. A PSA 10 sold for $641 in October, so the July number is already stale.

→ [1990 Bowman prices](/1990/bowman/)

## 6. 1990 Topps Tiffany #336 — PSA 9

**$625** best offer (Oct 4)

The only PSA 9 on this list, and it's here because a PSA 10 almost never comes up. Tiffany is the glossy, bright-backed version of the flagship Topps card — 361 PSA 9s exist and just 36 PSA 10s. When a 10 sells, it won't be anywhere near $625. (The PSA 9 has since gone to $689.)

→ [1990 Topps prices](/1990/topps/)

## 5. 1990 Topps Glossy Rookies #11 — PSA 10

**$700** best offer (Sep 4)

From the Topps Glossy Rookies insert set. PSA 10 population: 251. The seller had a rough time photographing this one — huge glare across the whole slab — and it still brought $700. Another PSA 10 sold for $675 on November 7, so that's the going rate.

→ [1990 Topps prices](/1990/topps/)

## 4. 1990 Donruss Diamond Kings #4 — PSA 10

**$850** best offer (Sep 10)

The Dick Perez painting. Diamond Kings are brutal on condition — those colored borders show every nick — so a 10 is genuinely hard. PSA 10 population: 114. This seller photographed it on a bedsheet, glare included, and it still sold for $850. Nothing has beaten that since.

→ [1990 Donruss prices](/1990/donruss/)

## 3. 1990 Topps Toys "R" Us Rookies #13 — PSA 10

**$1,000** buy it now (Oct 5)

Toys "R" Us did a yearly boxed rookie set of 30–35 cards, and this is Griffey's. PSA 10 population: 599, so it's not rare — a thousand dollars is a lot for the pop. Sure enough, the next one sold for $800 on October 28. Call it the most overheated card on the list.

→ [1990 Topps prices](/1990/topps/)

## 2. 1990 O-Pee-Chee #336 — PSA 10

**$1,170** on Fanatics Collect auction, 33 bids (Apr 27, 2025)

The Canadian version of the flagship Topps card. From the front it's nearly identical; flip it over and the back says "O-Pee-Chee, printed in Canada." This is the only sale on the list that isn't from 2026 — it hasn't sold in a 10 in a while. With Griffey prices up roughly 150% in the last year, if one came up today I'd expect well over this.

→ [1990 O-Pee-Chee prices](/1990/o-pee-chee/)

## 1. 1990 Topps #336 — PSA 10

**$1,450** best offer (Oct 4)

The flagship. Second-year Topps with the All-Star Rookie cup. The dark borders at the top make a centered, clean copy genuinely hard to find, which is why the most-printed Griffey card of 1990 is also the most expensive. It sold again for $1,509 at the end of October — so this one is still climbing.

→ [1990 Topps prices](/1990/topps/)

## What this list says about 1990

Nothing here is rare in the way a 90s insert is rare. Every card on this list has hundreds of PSA 10s, and most of them have thousands of graded copies. The prices come from two things: condition (dark Topps borders, Donruss red, Diamond Kings borders) and the Tiffany/O-Pee-Chee/Glossy variations that most people didn't know to keep. If you have a stack of 1990 Griffeys, the ones worth sending in are the ones with clean corners and perfect centering — and anything with a glossy back.
