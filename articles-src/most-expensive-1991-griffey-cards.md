Last week was 1990. This week it's 1991, and the top of this list is a different animal: three of the top three are Topps variations most collectors never saw in a pack. Here are the ten most expensive 1991 Griffey sales I track, ranked by price. All real eBay sales.

## 10. 1991 Fleer #450 "Bat Around .300" — PSA 10

**$137.50** on eBay auction (Oct 7)

The yellow set. There are two versions of the back: the error that says "Bat Around .300" and the corrected "Bat .300," and PSA now labels them separately. The error is the scarcer one — just 89 PSA 10s. Great photo of Griffey on the front, which is more than you can say for most of 1991 Fleer.

→ [1991 Fleer prices](/1991/fleer/)

## 9. 1991 Donruss MVP #392 — PSA 10

**$162.50** on eBay auction (Oct 13)

The MVP subset card. PSA 10 population: 222. Nothing fancy, it's just a clean 10 of a card everyone had, and those keep inching up — it sold for $175 in November.

→ [1991 Donruss prices](/1991/donruss/)

## 8. 1991 Score Dream Team #892 — PSA 10

**$192** best offer (Oct 14)

The black-and-white Dream Team insert from Score packs — one of the most recognizable Griffey cards of the era. PSA 10 population is 939, so it's not scarce, but it's iconic. (Softened to $135 a few weeks later, so $192 looks like the top for now.)

→ [1991 Score prices](/1991/score/)

## 7. 1991 Score All-Star #396 — PSA 10

**$222.50** on eBay auction (Oct 13)

The illustrated big-head All-Star card. PSA 10 population: 539. This one kept going after the video — it sold for $266 on November 6.

→ [1991 Score prices](/1991/score/)

## 6. 1991 O-Pee-Chee Premier #56 — PSA 10

**$230** best offer (Aug 4)

The Canadian premium set, and this copy is centered perfectly. Pop report: 686 graded, 280 PSA 9s, 79 PSA 10s. That's a tough ratio for a card that still sells for under $300 — one to watch.

→ [1991 O-Pee-Chee prices](/1991/o-pee-chee/)

## 5. 1991 Ultra Gold #4 — PSA 10

**$282** on eBay auction (Sep 19)

I own a few of these and they are very hard to gem. The backs are routinely off-center and the corners don't stay clean. A sharp PSA 10 like this one is the exception, and the price shows it.

→ [1991 Ultra prices](/1991/ultra/)

## 4. 1991 Studio #112 — PSA 10

**$380** best offer (Sep 23)

The first Studio set, with the portrait photography that made it famous. PSA 10 population: 174. I'll be honest — in the listing photo it almost looks like one corner has lifted. Could be the picture. I'd want to see this one in hand.

→ [1991 Studio prices](/1991/studio/)

## 3. 1991 Topps Desert Shield All-Star #392 — PSA 9

**$1,475** on eBay auction, 43 bids (Oct 11)

The All-Star card with the Desert Shield logo in the top right. These were shipped to troops in the Gulf, and they're tough — and increasingly faked. Look closely at the logo, and under a black light the real ones fluoresce at the corners of the back. A PSA 9 at $1,475 on 43 bids tells you how much demand there is for the real thing.

→ [1991 Topps prices](/1991/topps/)

## 2. 1991 Topps Tiffany #790 — PSA 10

**$1,655** on eBay auction, 41 bids (Oct 11)

The glossy factory-set version of the base card. PSA 10 population 102 out of 516 graded. This copy looks straight out of the pack, with a recent cert number — maybe a hair heavy left-to-right, but a beautiful card. Forty-one bids.

→ [1991 Topps prices](/1991/topps/)

## 1. 1991 Topps Desert Shield #790 — PSA 9

**$2,551** on eBay auction, 30 bids (Sep 13)

The base card with the Desert Shield stamp. Pop report: 200 PSA 9s, 28 PSA 10s. I don't have a recent PSA 10 sale to compare against, and I'm sure it would be a big number. Sharp corners on the back of this one, and 30 bidders agreed. This is the most expensive 1991 Griffey, and it isn't close.

→ [1991 Topps prices](/1991/topps/)

## What this list says about 1991

Seven of the ten sold for under $400. The other three are all 1991 Topps variations — Desert Shield All-Star #392 ($1,475), Tiffany #790 ($1,655) and Desert Shield #790 ($2,551) — and nothing else on the list is within $1,000 of them. For scale, the regular 1991 Topps #790 in a PSA 10 last sold for $201. Same photo, same design: the Tiffany gloss is worth about 8 times that, and the Desert Shield stamp about 12 times, in a lower grade. If you're going through 1991 Topps, check the front for the Desert Shield logo and the back for the glossy finish before you assume it's a common.
