Ten Griffey cards that sold between November 2 and 8 for significantly more than the previous sale of the same card in the same grade. Ranked by percent gain, smallest to biggest. Every number is a real eBay sale, and the "prior sale" is the last one I tracked at that grade.

## 10. 1993 SP #4 — PSA 10

**$521** on eBay auction, 26 bids (Nov 4) · prior sale **$425** (Sep 3) · **up 23%**

SP cards are tough to get in a 10 because there's no border — every edge is exposed, and any chipping shows. Two months between sales, almost a hundred dollars apart.

→ [1993 SP prices](/1993/sp/)

## 9. 1996 Metal Universe #107 — PSA 9

**$661** on eBay auction, 20 bids (Nov 2) · prior sale **$530** (Oct 11) · **up 25%**

Total PSA population 664, with 154 PSA 9s. One of the all-time great 90s base cards, and a 9 is a realistic grade for the foil. Up $131 in three weeks.

→ [1996 Metal Universe prices](/1996/metal-universe/)

## 8. 1997 Metal Universe Magnetic Field #4 — PSA 9

**$687** on eBay auction, 18 bids (Nov 4) · prior sale **$538** (Oct 28) · **up 28%**

Great Griffey image on the front and the magnetic-pattern back. The prior sale was one week earlier — so that's 28% in seven days.

→ [1997 Metal Universe prices](/1997/metal-universe/)

## 7. 1998 Finest #100 — PSA 10

**$225** (Nov 4) · prior sale **$175** (Aug 18) · **up 29%**

Just the base Finest card. Nothing special about the copy; the market for clean 10s of late-90s base cards keeps moving.

→ [1998 Finest prices](/1998/finest/)

## 2. 1999 Skybox Premium Soul of the Game #9SG — PSA 10

**$6,400** on eBay auction (Nov 6) · prior sale **$3,000** (May 16) · **up 113%**

A great-looking card and genuinely difficult in a 10 — PSA 10 population is just 35. The prior sale was back in May, so this is six months of market movement landing on one card. Only three bids on the auction, though, so it's one buyer's price rather than a bidding war.

→ [1999 Skybox Premium prices](/1999/skybox-premium/)

## 1. 1996 Collector's Choice You Crash the Game Gold — PSA 9

**$300** best offer (Nov 7) · prior sale **$140** (Oct 1) · **up 114%**

The biggest gainer of the week. Total PSA population is 10 — ten cards, five of them PSA 9s. The back looks like a redemption ticket, which is what it was: the Crash the Game cards were a contest, and the Gold version was the scarce one. From $140 to $300 in five weeks.

→ [1996 Collector's Choice prices](/1996/collectors-choice/)

## What this list says about the week

The headline is that cheap cards are moving as fast as expensive ones. Half of this list is under $400, and those cards posted the same 60–114% gains as the $6,400 Soul of the Game. The common thread is low population at the grade that sold — 35 PSA 10s, 51 PSA 8s, 5 PSA 9s — not the price tag. Overall the Griffey market was still soft this week; these ten were the exceptions.
