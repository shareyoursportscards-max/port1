10 Ken Griffey Jr. Cards Still Going Up in a Down Market

November 1, 2026 · 4 min read · real eBay sold prices

The sports card market is cooling off. Most of the Griffey cards I track dropped in October. These ten didn't. Ranked by percent gain over the previous sale, smallest to biggest. Every price is a real eBay sale; the "prior sale" is the last time that exact card sold in that exact grade.

10. 1992 Donruss Elite Series #13 — PSA 9

$998 on eBay auction (Oct 28) · prior sale $860 (Oct 4) · up 16%

One of the first serially numbered cards ever made. Nobody was numbering cards before 1992, and Donruss Elite was numbered to 10,000 — a huge print run by today's standards, but at the time it was the first "scarce" insert most collectors had ever pulled. Still a $1,000 card in a PSA 9 thirty-four years later.

9. 1993 Finest #110 — PSA 10

$2,550 on eBay auction (Oct 30) · prior sale $2,150 (Sep 23) · up 19%

The original Topps Finest. There are only 126 PSA 10s in the population report, which is why a base card from 1993 sells for the price of a used car. The Refractor version is a five-figure card; this is the one most of us can actually chase.

→ 1993 Finest prices

8. 1999 Topps Chrome Lords of the Diamond Refractor #LD1 — PSA 10

$7,500 best offer (Oct 29) · prior sale $5,750 (Oct 3) · up 30%

PSA 10 population: 45. That's the whole story. When fewer than fifty exist, one motivated buyer moves the price $1,750 in a month. Worth noting the PSA 9 went the other way the following week — down to $1,400 — so this is a 10-only phenomenon.

→ 1999 Topps Chrome prices

7. 1994 Pinnacle The Naturals #3 — PSA 9

$159 on eBay auction (Oct 27) · prior sale $120 (Oct 13) · up 33%

Beautiful card, super sharp in person, and genuinely hard from a condition standpoint — the edges chip if you look at them wrong. Population in PSA 9 is 228 out of 491 total graded, so a 9 is the realistic top grade for most copies. Even this one has a small nick on the top-left corner of the back.

→ 1994 Pinnacle prices

6. 1990 Donruss Learning Series #8 — PSA 9

$135 buy it now (Oct 29) · prior sale $95 (Aug 22) · up 42%

A rare junk-wax card, which sounds like a contradiction. Learning Series was a small factory set, not a pack pull, so there are far fewer of these than the regular 1990 Donruss #365. Second-year Griffey cards in general are getting more attention this fall — this is the first of three on this list.

→ 1990 Donruss prices

5. 1997 Flair Showcase Diamond Cuts #5 — PSA 10

$2,951 on eBay auction (Oct 30) · prior sale $2,000 (Aug 12) · up 48%

Die-cut, crystal clear, and one of the best-looking inserts of the decade. The seller on this one posted a video of the card from every angle, which I'd love to see become standard on any card over $1,000. Nearly a thousand-dollar jump in two and a half months.

→ 1997 Flair Showcase prices

4. 1995 Pinnacle #128 "Bubble Gum" — PSA 10

$4,504 best offer (Oct 31) · prior sale $3,050 (Oct 4) · up 48%

The iconic Bubble Gum card. It's technically a base card, but with a PSA 10 population of about 104 it trades like an insert. This is the card people who don't collect Griffey still recognize. It sold again for $4,000 the following week, so call it a $4,000–$4,500 card right now, not a one-off.

→ 1995 Pinnacle prices

3. 1993 Score #504 — PSA 10

$165.50 on eBay auction (Oct 28) · prior sale $110 (Oct 4) · up 50%

The animated All-Star insert — the cartoon Griffey. PSA 10 population of 150. Great edges on this copy and the image is sharp. A fun, cheap card that's quietly 50% more expensive than it was a month ago.

→ 1993 Score prices

2. 1990 Donruss Diamond Kings #4 — PSA 9

$100 best offer (Oct 28) · prior sale $51 (Oct 7) · up 96%

Second-year Griffey, Dick Perez painting, and it just doubled. Three weeks between sales. Diamond Kings from 1990 were in every pack and every shoebox, which is exactly why clean PSA 9s are getting harder to find than people assume.

→ 1990 Donruss prices

1. 1990 Fleer Soaring Stars #6 — PSA 8

$100 buy it now (Oct 31) · prior sale $50 (Aug 9) · up 100%

The biggest percentage jump of the month, and it's a PSA 8. Total PSA population is 551, so there are 9s and 10s out there that should be selling for more — if you own one, this is your sign to check comps. Third second-year card on this list, and the pattern is clear: 1990 Griffey is having a moment.

→ 1990 Fleer prices

What this list says about the market

Gains ran from 16% to 100%. Five of the ten sold for under $200 and four sold for over $2,000, so the moves weren't limited to one price range. Three of the ten are 1990 cards — Learning Series, Diamond Kings and Soaring Stars — and the last two of those were the biggest gainers on the list, at 96% and 100%.
