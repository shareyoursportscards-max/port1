1994September 25, 2026

1994 Flair Hot Gloves #3 was the year's busiest high-end card with six raw sales between $295 and $499, easing from $450 to $411, while a PSA 10 sold for $2,200. A raw 1994 Finest Refractor #232 sold for $1,650, up from $1,250 (up 32%). 1994 SP Holoview Blue FX #12 PSA 9 climbed from $130 to $225 (up 73%). Lower down, Ultra Home Run Kings #2 came back to earth across seven raw sales, from $42 to $29.

1995September 25, 2026

The 1995 Pinnacle #128 bubble gum card was the busiest Griffey card the guide has ever tracked in one week — forty-four raw sales between $38 and $85, settling from $63 to $54. Its graded tiers held up far better: PSA 9 traded five times between $500 and $650, and the Museum Collection parallel took 72 bids to $1,107 in PSA 9. 1995 Leaf Limited Gold #6 PSA 10 sold for $1,500, up from $420, one of the largest single moves of the week. 1995 Pinnacle White Hot #WH2 PSA 9 climbed from $1,300 to $1,726 on a 34-bid auction.

1996September 25, 2026

1996 Ultra Thunderclap #11 nearly doubled, $1,300 to $2,550 raw (up 96%) after a 30-bid auction — the strongest '96 insert result of the week. Metal Universe pulled in two directions: the 1996 Metal Universe #107 base card was the busiest Griffey card of the week with twenty raw sales between $50 and $120, easing from $79 to $75, while its Platinum Edition parallel climbed from $225 to $242 across seven sales. The set's Heavy Metal #4 went the other way entirely, down from $52 to $26 (down 50%) across thirteen sales that never once cleared $37.

1997September 25, 2026

The 1997 Bowman's Best Best Cuts Atomic Refractor #BC6 went from $475 to $1,225 raw (up 158%) across three sales, one of them a 32-bid auction that closed at $1,225 and another at $1,500 — the Atomic has pulled well clear of the standard Best Cuts refractor, which still trades at $320. 1997 Metal Universe Blast Furnace #6 PSA 9 climbed from $215 to $615 (up 186%) on a 16-bid auction, and its PSA 10 sold for $1,227 after 38 bids. The 1997 Metal Universe #145 base card was the busiest Griffey card of the week with twenty-two raw sales between $23.50 and $45, ten of them contested auctions; the price eased from $41 to $33.

1999September 24, 2026
